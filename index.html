      6. Inotropes Infusion Calculator

      Educational calculation only — not for prescribing, preparing, or administering medication.

      Formula: mL/hr = (mcg/kg/min × kg × 60 × final mL) ÷ (total mg × 1000).

      Select a medication to view general administration safety instructions.

      7. APGAR Score — Detailed Newborn Assessment

      Choose the observed clinical description, not a numeric score. Assess at 1 and 5 minutes after birth; document further assessments as clinically indicated.

      Complete the five assessments and select the time.

      APGAR documents newborn condition and response to care; it must never delay or determine initiation of neonatal resuscitation. A low score requires immediate clinical assessment according to neonatal protocols.

      8. Intake & Output — Fluid Balance

      Enter totals for the same documented time period. All volumes are in mL.

      Color key: 🟢 Green = positive balance (intake exceeds output); 🔴 Red = negative balance (output exceeds intake); ⚪ White = zero balance. Colors describe arithmetic only, NOT whether a result is clinically safe or normal. Interpret with patient condition, orders, time period and local policy.
      Educational use only. Not validated for clinical decisions or medication administration. Verify orders, units, protocols, and independent checks as required. GCS components must be assessed appropriately; do not total untestable components.
